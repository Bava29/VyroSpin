YOUR FIRST RIDE STARTS HERE

SHOW UP READY.
RIDE WITH CONFIDENCE.

New to indoor cycling? No pressure. A little preparation helps you feel comfortable, enjoy the music and focus on your own progress.

ARRIVE 10–15 MINUTES EARLY

Give yourself time to check in, meet your coach and get your bike adjusted.

BRING WATER & A TOWEL

Keep water nearby and bring a small towel to stay comfortable throughout your session.

WEAR COMFORTABLE WORKOUT GEAR

Choose breathable clothing and suitable athletic footwear for indoor cycling.

GO AT YOUR OWN PACE

Follow the coach's guidance, take breaks when needed and adjust the resistance to your comfort.
