Which package should I choose as a beginner?

Start with a single drop-in class to experience the studio before committing to a larger package. You can explore other options after your first ride.

How do I book a class using my package?

Check the class schedule, select your preferred session and follow the booking process. Confirm how package credits are applied when booking.

Do my class credits expire?

Package validity depends on the plan you purchase. Check the expiry date and usage conditions displayed before checkout.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Cancellation and rescheduling depend on the studio's booking policy. Review the applicable deadline and any credit or fee conditions before confirming.

Does a monthly membership include unlimited classes?

Not necessarily. The number of included classes depends on the membership selected. Check the monthly allowance and restrictions before purchase.

Can I change my membership later?

Changes depend on the studio's membership terms. Contact the team to check whether an upgrade, downgrade or plan change is available.
