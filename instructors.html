NEW TO SPINNING? WE'VE GOT YOU.

Your first indoor cycling class doesn't have to feel intimidating. Here's everything you need to know to arrive prepared, feel comfortable and enjoy your ride.

FIRST CLASS CHECKLIST

SHOW UP.
SETTLE IN.
FIND YOUR RHYTHM.

No experience needed. Just start at your own pace.

01

ARRIVE 10–15 MINUTES EARLY

Allow time to check in, settle your belongings and ask your instructor about the bike setup. Check the studio's arrival policy before booking.

02

CHOOSE THE RIGHT GEAR

Wear breathable workout clothes and suitable athletic footwear. Confirm whether your studio provides cycling shoes or requires specific shoes.

03

TELL YOUR INSTRUCTOR

Let your coach know you're new. Ask for help adjusting your seat, handlebars and resistance before the class begins.

04

RIDE AT YOUR OWN PACE

You don't need to match the person next to you. Adjust your resistance, take breaks when needed and ask your coach if you need guidance.

05

BRING YOUR ESSENTIALS

Pack a water bottle and small towel. Follow studio guidance for hydration, equipment and storing personal belongings.

YOUR COMFORT COMES FIRST

It's okay to slow down or rest. Stop exercising if you experience dizziness, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath, and seek appropriate medical help.
